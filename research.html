Work in progress

“A Structural Theory of the Willingness-to-Pay Gap: Valuation and Behaviour in Climate Mitigation”, 2026

This paper studies why stated willingness to pay for climate mitigation may differ from actual contribution behaviour. I develop a simple microeconomic model in which environmental valuation, warm-glow motives, and social-image concerns jointly shape climate-related giving.

“Kantian Belief Equilibrium and Collective Pivotality in Threshold Public Good Games”, 2026

This paper develops a belief-based model of Kantian reasoning in threshold public-good games. Instead of assuming universal cooperation or standard Nash reasoning, the model studies how agents’ beliefs about like-minded others can sustain, weaken, or collapse cooperative behaviour.

“Towards a Theory of Explanatory Closure: A Map of the Boundary”, 2026

This paper explores the structure of rational explanation from a metaphysical perspective. Rather than defending a particular ultimate ground, it asks what limits and closure conditions explanatory inquiry must face if rational investigation is possible at all.
